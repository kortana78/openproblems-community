# OpenProblems community

Public discussion space for [OpenProblems](https://openproblems.vercel.app), an independent catalogue of open questions.

This repository holds discussion threads only. The catalogue source code stays private.

## What belongs here

- Corrections to a catalogue entry
- Missing or better primary sources
- Questions about a problem statement or its status
- Evidence for or against a claimed proof

## What does not belong here

- Spam, promotion or unrelated links
- Claims without a source or a short explanation
- Personal information about other people

## Status labels are editorial

A comment, a claimed proof or a link posted here does not change the status of a catalogue entry. Claims remain claims until the relevant expert community accepts them. Catalogue status changes require editorial review and a dated record.

## Moderation

Threads may be edited, locked or removed. Repeated spam or abusive behaviour will be blocked.

The public site is at https://openproblems.vercel.app. The Proof Lab is at https://openproblems.vercel.app/formal.
